NovaPlayer · Privacy

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what NovaPlayer accesses, why it uses that data, where the data is stored, when it is shared, and how you can delete it or revoke access.

Last updated: 14 November 2026
Applies to: NovaPlayer 1.0 and later on iOS, iPadOS, macOS and tvOS.

English 简体中文

The short version

  • NovaPlayer works without a NovaPlayer account.
  • Your media files are not uploaded to a developer-operated server and are never sold.
  • Google Drive is accessed only after you choose to connect it. NovaPlayer requests read-only Drive access, never write access.
  • Google user data is not used for advertising, profiling, sale, or AI training.
  • NovaPlayer does not generate or edit images and does not create or distribute AI-generated NCII.
  • Source credentials are kept in Apple Keychain. Optional Pro sync uses your private iCloud database controlled by your Apple Account.

English

NovaPlayer Privacy Policy

1. Scope and responsibility

This policy applies to the NovaPlayer application for iOS, iPadOS, macOS and tvOS and to the website at /novaplayer/. NovaPlayer is the app identified in the App Store as com.novaplayer.ios. References to “NovaPlayer,” “we,” or “us” mean the developer distributing this app. Questions or privacy requests can be submitted through the NovaPlayer support page.

NovaPlayer is a player for media you own or are authorized to access. It does not provide media, operate a media relay, or bypass DRM. You choose every local, network, server or cloud source that the app can access.

Google API disclosure

2. How NovaPlayer accesses Google user data

NovaPlayer contacts Google only after you select Google Drive and complete Google’s authorization screen. The permission you grant is read-only. NovaPlayer does not request Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, Google profile, email address, or identity scopes.

Platform Google scope What it permits
iOS, iPadOS and macOS drive.readonly Read-only access to Google Drive file metadata and content so NovaPlayer can build and play your library. NovaPlayer cannot create, edit, move, share or delete Drive files with this scope.
tvOS drive.file Access limited to Google Drive files that Google makes available to NovaPlayer under this scope. It does not grant drive-wide read-only access.

Google data NovaPlayer reads

Depending on the source and the operation you request, NovaPlayer may read:

  • Google Drive file identifiers, file and folder names, MIME type, size, modification date, parent folder identifiers and trashed state;
  • video file content, through byte-range requests, when you browse or play a selected video;
  • sidecar files such as NFO metadata, posters, fanart and backdrop images, when present;
  • Google OAuth access and refresh tokens returned after you authorize the app; and
  • pagination and change-notification tokens needed to scan efficiently and detect changed, removed or renamed items.

How NovaPlayer uses Google user data

  • Authenticate your Google Drive connection and refresh expired access tokens;
  • list and index supported media and artwork files that you have chosen to make available;
  • present titles, artwork, folder relationships, progress and source information in your library;
  • stream the media you select and support resuming playback;
  • perform incremental rescans and identify files that were added, changed, removed or renamed; and
  • diagnose failures using stable error codes while masking credentials and private paths.

Limits on use

NovaPlayer does not use Google user data for advertising, personalized or retargeted marketing, profiling, eligibility decisions, credit or lending decisions, sale, data brokerage, or training generalized AI or machine-learning models. We do not allow humans to read your Google user data except when necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or when you explicitly request support that requires it.

AI and NCII prohibition

NovaPlayer does not provide generative image or video creation features. It does not create, alter, or distribute AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII), and it does not use Google user data to train, fine-tune, or operate generative AI or machine-learning models. This prohibition applies to every use of NovaPlayer and to all data obtained through Google APIs.

Google Limited Use

NovaPlayer’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage, sharing and retention

Temporary playback cache is replaced during normal use and can be removed by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app.

Sharing and third-party services

  • Google: NovaPlayer sends authenticated API requests directly to Google to list files, refresh authorization and stream selected media. Google receives the information necessary to respond to those requests under Google’s own privacy policy.
  • TMDB: To retrieve artwork and descriptive metadata, NovaPlayer may send a media title derived from a filename and related year or episode information to TMDB. It does not send Google OAuth tokens, your Google account identity, Google file identifiers, or media file content to TMDB.
  • Apple: If you enable Pro sync or credential pairing, Apple stores the synced data in your private iCloud database. Apple processes App Store purchases and other Apple services used by the app.
  • Your configured sources: Connection details and credentials may be sent only to the server or cloud provider you explicitly configure.

We do not sell Google user data, share it with advertising networks or data brokers, or disclose it to any other third party for an unrelated purpose. We may disclose information if required by applicable law, but only to the extent legally required and subject to appropriate safeguards.

Security

NovaPlayer uses platform transport security for network requests, Apple Keychain for source credentials, the app sandbox for local storage, protected file permissions for rebuildable scan data, and logging rules that mask credentials and private paths. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

Control and deletion

4. Deleting data and revoking access

  1. Delete the Google Drive source in NovaPlayer. Open Settings → Media Sources & Network → Sources, select the Google Drive source and delete it. NovaPlayer removes the related Keychain credential and local source data through its normal deletion flow.
  2. Revoke Google access. Visit Google Account permissions and remove NovaPlayer. Revoking access prevents future Drive API requests, although a copy may remain on your device until NovaPlayer removes the source or credential.
  3. Delete Pro sync data. Disable Pro sync and use NovaPlayer’s “Clear iCloud Data” control. You can also manage the app’s iCloud data in Apple iCloud settings.
  4. Uninstall NovaPlayer. This removes the app container and its local database, but Apple does not guarantee that Keychain items are removed. Delete cloud sources inside NovaPlayer before uninstalling if you want their stored credentials removed.
  5. Contact us. If you cannot find or delete data through the controls above, use the support page. Do not post passwords, OAuth tokens, private media URLs or file contents publicly.

5. Changes to this policy

We review this policy when NovaPlayer’s data practices change. If a change materially affects how Google user data is accessed, used, stored, retained or shared, we will update the “Last updated” date and provide a notice in NovaPlayer before the change takes effect. Where required by Google policy or applicable law, we will request renewed consent before the new use begins.

6. Tracking, AI safeguards, children and contact

NovaPlayer does not use Google user data or other app data for advertising or cross-app tracking. The app does not contain developer-operated advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. NovaPlayer does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children, and is rated 4+.

NovaPlayer does not generate or edit images and must not be used to create, modify or distribute AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery. The app does not use Google API data to train or operate generative AI models.

Privacy questions and requests can be submitted through the NovaPlayer support page.

NovaPlayer 隐私政策

1. 适用范围与责任主体

本政策适用于 iOS、iPadOS、macOS、tvOS 上的 NovaPlayer 应用及 /novaplayer/ 网站。NovaPlayer 在 App Store 中的 Bundle ID 为 com.novaplayer.ios。本政策中的“NovaPlayer”“我们”指发布该应用的开发者。隐私问题或数据请求可通过支持页提交。

NovaPlayer 仅用于播放你拥有或已获授权访问的媒体，不提供媒体内容、不运营媒体中继服务，也不绕过 DRM。所有本地目录、网络共享、媒体服务器或云盘来源均由你主动添加。

Google API 数据披露

2. NovaPlayer 如何访问 Google 用户数据

只有当你选择 Google Drive 并完成 Google 授权页面后，NovaPlayer 才会访问 Google 数据。授予应用的权限为只读。NovaPlayer 不申请 Gmail、日历、联系人、Google 个人资料、电子邮箱或身份权限。

平台 Google scope 授权范围
iOS、iPadOS、macOS drive.readonly 只读访问 Google Drive 文件元数据与内容，用于建立媒体库并播放媒体。该 scope 不允许 NovaPlayer 创建、修改、移动、共享或删除 Drive 文件。
tvOS drive.file 仅访问 Google 按该 scope 提供给 NovaPlayer 的 Drive 文件，不授予整个 Drive 的只读访问权限。

NovaPlayer 会读取哪些 Google 数据

根据你使用的来源和操作，NovaPlayer 可能读取：

  • Google Drive 文件标识、文件与文件夹名称、MIME 类型、大小、修改时间、父文件夹标识及回收站状态；
  • 当你浏览或播放选定视频时，通过字节范围请求读取视频内容；
  • 来源中存在的 NFO 元数据、海报、fanart 与背景图等附属文件；
  • 你完成授权后 Google 返回的 OAuth access token 与 refresh token；
  • 用于分页扫描、增量变化检测和识别新增、修改、删除或重命名文件的游标与变更令牌。

Google 用户数据的用途

  • 验证 Google Drive 连接并在 access token 过期后刷新授权；
  • 列出并索引你选择提供的受支持媒体与海报文件；
  • 在媒体库中展示标题、海报、文件夹关系、播放进度与来源信息；
  • 播放你选择的媒体并支持继续播放；
  • 执行增量扫描，识别新增、修改、删除或重命名的文件；
  • 使用稳定错误码排查故障，同时遮蔽凭据与私密路径。

禁止用途

NovaPlayer 不会将 Google 用户数据用于广告、个性化或再营销、用户画像、资格判断、信用或贷款决策、出售、数据经纪，也不会用于训练通用 AI 或机器学习模型。除安全防护、欺诈防范、遵守法律或你明确请求且处理支持问题所必需的情形外，我们不允许人工读取你的 Google 用户数据。

禁止 AI 与 NCII 用途

NovaPlayer 不提供生成式图像或视频创作功能，不会创建、修改或传播 AI 生成的未经当事人同意的私密图像（NCII），也不会使用 Google 用户数据训练、微调或运行生成式 AI 或机器学习模型。该禁令适用于 NovaPlayer 的所有使用场景及通过 Google API 获得的所有数据。

Google Limited Use

NovaPlayer 对从 Google API 获得的信息的使用与向其他应用转移，遵守 Google API Services User Data Policy，包括 Limited Use 要求。

安全措施

NovaPlayer 使用平台网络安全机制传输请求，使用 Apple 钥匙串保存来源凭据，通过 App 沙盒保存本地数据，对可重建扫描数据应用受保护的文件权限，并在日志中遮蔽凭据与私密路径。任何存储或传输方式都无法保证绝对安全，但我们会采取合理措施保护数据。

控制与删除

4. 删除数据与撤销授权

  1. 在 NovaPlayer 中删除 Google Drive 来源。进入「设置 → 媒体源与网络 → 来源」，选择 Google Drive 来源并删除。NovaPlayer 会通过正常删除流程移除相关钥匙串凭据和本地来源数据。
  2. 撤销 Google 授权。访问 Google 账号权限页面并移除 NovaPlayer。撤销后将无法继续发起 Drive API 请求；设备端副本可能在删除来源或凭据前仍然存在。
  3. 删除 Pro 同步数据。关闭 Pro 同步并使用 NovaPlayer 的「清除 iCloud 数据」功能，也可在 Apple iCloud 设置中管理 App 的 iCloud 数据。
  4. 卸载 NovaPlayer。这会移除 App 容器及本地数据库，但 Apple 不保证卸载会清除钥匙串条目。如需删除云盘凭据，请先在 App 内删除云盘来源。
  5. 联系我们。如果无法通过上述功能找到或删除数据，请使用支持页。请勿公开发布密码、OAuth token、私密媒体 URL 或文件内容。

5. 政策变更

当 NovaPlayer 的数据处理方式发生变化时，我们会审查本政策。如果变更会实质影响 Google 用户数据的访问、使用、保存、保留或共享方式，我们会更新“最后更新”日期，并在变更生效前通过 NovaPlayer 发出通知。若 Google 政策或适用法律要求，我们会在新用途开始前重新征得你的同意。

6. 跟踪、AI 保护、儿童与联系我们

NovaPlayer 不会将 Google 用户数据或其他 App 数据用于广告或跨 App 跟踪。App 不包含开发者运营的广告、分析或崩溃上报 SDK。NovaPlayer 不会有意收集任何人的个人数据（包括儿童），年龄分级为 4+。

NovaPlayer 不生成或编辑图像，也不得用于创建、修改或传播 AI 生成的未经当事人同意的私密图像。App 不使用 Google API 数据训练或运行生成式 AI 模型。

隐私问题或数据请求请通过NovaPlayer 支持页提交。
