Privacy without fine print

Your media stays where you put it.

NovaPlayer works without a NovaPlayer account. Your media files are not uploaded to the developer, and the app does not include developer-operated advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Read the full Privacy Policy →

Your files stay at the source

Media remains in the folder, server or cloud drive you configured. Playback requests go directly to that source.

Cloud access is your choice

If you connect a cloud drive such as Google Drive, NovaPlayer requests access to the Drive data needed to browse, organize and play media you select inside the app.

Credentials stay protected

Server passwords and cloud OAuth tokens are stored in Apple Keychain and sent only to the server or provider you configure.

No ads or cross-app tracking

NovaPlayer does not sell your data, use it for advertising or cross-app tracking, or send your media files to the developer.

How connected cloud data is used

Only to make the library you asked for work.

When you authorize a cloud provider, NovaPlayer uses that access to list selected media, read the metadata needed for your library, stream files you choose and retain scan checkpoints. It does not use Google user data for advertising, sell it, or send it to the developer. NovaPlayer’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

AI and prohibited use

No AI image generation. No NCII.

NovaPlayer does not provide image generation or image editing features. It does not create, alter, or distribute AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII) and does not use Google user data to train or operate generative AI models. This prohibition applies to every use of NovaPlayer and to all data obtained through Google APIs.
