No account. No subscription. No developer server.

Tap to record a meeting or hold to capture a quick thought. SpeakKeep turns it into an editable, searchable note, suggests tags and action items, and keeps every passage aligned with its audio.

What SpeakKeep does

Capture it your way

Tap for longer sessions or hold for quick thoughts. Import existing recordings through the system share sheet or file picker, then work with them exactly like notes recorded in the app.

Turn speech into something you can search

Transcribe in ten languages, search transcripts, tags and titles, and jump from any sentence to that moment in the recording. Spotlight can find your notes too.

Keep the useful parts

Get a summary, tag suggestions and action items from your own words. Tags reuse wording you already use, and nothing becomes a task until you confirm it.

Edit without losing the source

Fix a sentence while the original transcript and timestamps stay intact. Continue a recording, trim a clip, or remove a middle section into a new note.

Share without leaving the app

Copy text, share audio or MP3, create a shareable image, or batch export notes as TXT, Markdown or PDF.

Keep your notes private

Recordings and text stay on your device. There is no analytics, advertising or developer server. iCloud sync is optional, off by default, and stays in your own private iCloud account.

中文介绍

SpeakKeep 把随口说出的话变成可搜索、可整理的语音笔记。录音、转写、搜索与整理都在你的设备上完成，不需要账号，也没有开发者服务器。

轻点适合会议、讲座和长口述，按住适合闪念、待办和灵感；已有录音也可以通过系统分享面板或文件选择器导入。转写支持十种语言，文字与音频逐段对齐，点任意一句即可从那一刻播放。转写完成后会自动生成摘要、标签建议和行动项建议；行动项只有在你确认之后才会创建。

你可以编辑转写、继续录制或修剪音频，也能按标签与时间范围批量导出 TXT、Markdown 和 PDF。iCloud 私有同步随买断提供、默认关闭，数据始终留在你自己的 iCloud 账户中。

支持 iPhone、iPad（iOS / iPadOS 18 或更高版本）与 Mac（macOS 14 或更高版本）。
